For developing AI learning software, North Rhine-Westphalia pays up to 15,000 euros, Bavaria up to 7,500 euros and, once, up to 30,000 euros for particularly innovative projects. Yet most searches for e-learning funding in Germany end at training subsidies, and those pay for courses, not software.

This is for companies with a German entity and training providers in Germany. All figures come from the funding bodies' guidelines (in German), as of November 2026.

Which of the two fits depends on the purpose. NRW funds products for the market, such as a learning platform a training provider sells to clients. Bavaria funds solutions for your own site, such as an [AI learning companion](/blog/custom-ai-tutor-development) for your apprentices. What such a project costs is covered in [custom e-learning development costs](/blog/custom-e-learning-development-costs).

## MID-Digitalisierung in North Rhine-Westphalia

Key points from the [guideline](https://recht.nrw.de/mbnrw/2026-10/), valid until the end of 2029:

- **Who:** SMEs registered in NRW that are not linked to the contractor.
- **What:** the concept, prototype or further development of a digital product using a key technology such as AI. It must be new or clearly improve on the state of the art, and be demonstrably intended for the market.
- **How much:** 50 percent of net costs, up to 15,000 euros. The grant must be at least 4,000 euros, so the project must cost at least 8,000 euros net.
- **Who builds it:** a provider of your choice with a registered seat in the EU, no certification needed, but no subcontractors or freelancers either.
- **Process:** register on the funding portal, a monthly lottery if there are many applicants, then the full application. Once every two years.

The product must address a specific target group and have realistic market prospects. A learning tool only for your own apprentices does not meet this.

## Digitalbonus in Bavaria

Key points from the [guideline](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/BayVV_7071_W_14585/True), valid until the end of 2027:

- **Who:** commercial companies under 50 full-time equivalents with no more than 10 million euros in turnover or balance sheet total, with a site in Bavaria where the solution is used. No liberal professions.
- **What:** developing, introducing or improving products, services and processes, for example with AI. It funds work by external providers including the software needed. Standard software such as conventional office software does not count.
- **How much:** up to 50 percent, from 4,000 euros in costs. Up to 7,500 euros, or once up to 30,000 euros in the Plus track for particularly innovative projects.
- **Process:** you apply yourself with your ELSTER business account. Service providers may not do it for you.

## Apply before you order

<FundingTimeline lang="en" />

In short: get a non-binding quote, apply, wait for the go-ahead, and only then sign the order. In Bavaria you may start once the application receipt is confirmed. In NRW, check the timing with NRW.BANK.

## What is not funded

- **Standard software** such as conventional office software in the Bavarian Digitalbonus.
- **Learning tools only for your own company** in MID-Digitalisierung, where the product must be intended for the market.
- **Solutions you also want to sell to other companies** in the Bavarian Digitalbonus, if they would be fundable there too (No. 4.3 of the guideline).
- **Software and licences** in the sister programme MID-Digitale Prozesse, which only funds consulting ([NRW.BANK](https://www.nrwbank.de/mid-digitale-prozesse)).
- **Projects that are already ordered.**

## What about the Federal Employment Agency?

Under [§ 82 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__82.html) it funds training for your employees in courses by approved providers with more than 120 hours. The money goes to the training provider, not to a software vendor.

Training on company-specific software is not eligible, according to the [agency](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/finanziell/foerderung-von-weiterbildung). What that means for providers is covered in [learning platform for training providers](/blog/learning-platform-training-providers).

## Other German states

Other states fund SME digitalisation too, with their own rules. You can search for more programmes in the [funding database](https://www.foerderdatenbank.de) of the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (in German).
