"Free" can mean two things for training logbook software and apps: a plan that stays free as long as you stay within its limits, or a time-limited trial after which a subscription starts. For a tool your apprentices use for two to three years, that is a big difference.

Companies whose apprentices use the IHK's digital Berichtsheft have to choose now: it ends on 31 December 2026, according to several IHKs ([what that means](/blog/digital-training-logbook#what-happens-to-the-ihk-digital-logbook)). The company pays for the new solution, not the apprentice ([§ 14 (1) no. 3 BBiG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bbig_2005/__14.html)).

## What a Trial Shows and What It Does Not

A trial is useful for trying out how the software works. Some points only show up over the course of the apprenticeship, though. So clarify them with the provider beforehand:

<TrialVsTrainingBar lang="en" />

- **The record must be complete.** Without it there is no admission to the final examination ([§ 43 (1) no. 2 BBiG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bbig_2005/__43.html)). If you do not subscribe after the trial, the part written so far still has to be kept.
- **Switching takes work.** Exporting, making a clean cut, presenting two parts for the exam. That is exactly what companies whose apprentices use the IHK tool are facing right now.
- **Costs grow.** Calculate the price for the number of apprentices you will be training at the same time in three years, not for today.

## 9 Questions to Ask Every Provider

Ask every provider the same questions. The most important come first: export and data protection.

<CopyBlock buttonLabel="Copy questions" copiedLabel="Copied!">

### Checklist: Comparing Training Logbook Software

**Data**

- [ ] Can we get all records as a PDF at any time, also after cancelling or changing plans?
- [ ] Is there a data processing agreement, also on the free plan?
- [ ] Where is the data hosted: Germany, the EU or elsewhere?

**Costs**

- [ ] What happens with the next apprentice if we go over a limit?
- [ ] What does the free plan include, and what applies only during the trial?
- [ ] How is billing done, and with which term and notice period?

**Practice**

- [ ] Does the entry form cover the minimum requirements of the BIBB recommendation, such as company activities, instruction and vocational school topics?
- [ ] Does the trainer's approval appear in the record with name and date?
- [ ] Are there reminders for missing entries?

</CopyBlock>

Clarify first: which form of record does your chamber accept? IHK Hannover, for example, accepts other providers' products if they meet the [BIBB recommendation](https://www.bibb.de/dokumente/pdf/HA156.pdf) ([IHK Hannover](https://www.ihk.de/hannover/hauptnavigation/ausbildung-und-weiterbildung/ausbildung/ausbildung-a-z/digitales-berichtsheft-5184974)). ## When Is a Subscription Worth It?

For a few apprentices, a free route is often enough, including the BIBB samples on paper or in Word. A subscription pays off more when you train many apprentices or need more than the record, such as [exam preparation](/blog/ihk-exam-preparation-digital). Then ask whether the logbook stays usable on its own.
