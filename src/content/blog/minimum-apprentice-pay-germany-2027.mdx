From 1 January 2027, Germany's statutory minimum pay for apprentices (*Mindestausbildungsvergütung*) rises to **764 euros** per month in the first year. The new rates have been in the Federal Law Gazette since 7 October 2026. For budgeting, that one number is not enough, because one detail is easy to miss: the floor depends on the **year the apprenticeship started**, not on the calendar year. An apprentice who started in August 2026 does not move to the new table in 2027.

This article shows the 2027 rates next to 2026, how they are calculated, who the floor actually affects, and what an apprenticeship starting in 2027 costs at minimum.

## The 2027 Rates at a Glance

Monthly minimum pay under Section 17 (2) BBiG for apprenticeships starting between 1 January and 31 December 2027 ([BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 287](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bbimindvergfbek_2027/BJNR11F0A0026.html)), compared with the previous year ([2026 announcement](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bbimindvergfbek_2026/BJNR0EB0A0025.html)):

| Training year | Start 2026 | Start 2027 | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | €724 | **€764** | +€40 |
| Year 2 | €854 | **€902** | +€48 |
| Year 3 | €977 | **€1,031** | +€54 |
| Year 4 | €1,014 | **€1,070** | +€56 |

[BIBB](https://www.bibb.de/de/pressemitteilung_229684.php) puts the year-one increase at just under 5.6 percent.

## The Start Year Decides

Section 17 (2) BBiG states that the updated amount applies to apprenticeships **that begin in the year of the update**. The uplifts for years two to four are calculated from that amount. Each intake therefore keeps its own table.

An example for a company that hires every August. In September 2027, these floors apply:

| Intake | Training year in Sept. 2027 | Statutory floor |
|---|---|---|
| Started August 2027 | Year 1 | €764 (2027 table) |
| Started August 2026 | Year 2 | €854 (2026 table) |

The second-year apprentice has a lower floor than the 2027 table shows for year two (€902). This is only the legal minimum. Whatever the apprenticeship contract or a collective agreement says applies regardless, and many companies pay more anyway. For planning, this means: new contracts starting in 2027 must meet the new rates, and the announcement does not change existing contracts.

## How the Amount Is Calculated

Since 2024, the year-one amount is updated every year (Section 17 (2) BBiG):

- **Basis:** how average apprentice pay developed over the two calendar years before the announcement. BIBB has done the calculation since 2023.
- **Uplifts:** the year-one amount plus 18 percent for year two, 35 percent for year three and 40 percent for year four. That gives 902, 1,031 and 1,070 euros.
- **Publication:** the responsible federal ministry publishes the rates in the Federal Law Gazette by 1 November at the latest. For 2027 this happened on 7 October 2026.

The next update, for apprenticeships starting in 2028, will therefore be published by 1 November 2027 at the latest. If you advertise 2028 places in spring 2027, you will not know the floor yet.

## Who the Floor Actually Affects

For most companies, the statutory minimum is not the number on the payslip. According to [BIBB](https://www.bibb.de/de/pressemitteilung_229684.php), only three to four percent of new apprenticeship contracts since 2020 were agreed at the minimum in year one. Apprentices in companies bound by collective agreements earned 1,209 euros gross per month on average across all training years in 2025.

Which limit applies to your company depends on whether you are bound by a collective agreement:

- **Bound by a collective agreement:** pay set by an applicable agreement counts as appropriate even if it is below the minimum (Section 17 (3) BBiG).
- **Not bound:** even above the floor, pay is as a rule not appropriate if it falls more than 20 percent below the collective agreement that covers the apprenticeship (Section 17 (4) BBiG). For many companies, this is the higher limit.
- **Part-time apprenticeships:** pay may be reduced, but not by a higher percentage than working time (Section 17 (5) BBiG). At 75 percent of normal hours, that means at least 573 euros in year one for a 2027 start.
- **Benefits in kind:** can be counted at the official social-security values, but not beyond 75 percent of gross pay (Section 17 (6) BBiG).

Your chamber (IHK or Handwerkskammer) can tell you which collective agreement is relevant for your sector and region.

## What an Apprenticeship Starting in 2027 Costs at Minimum

The statutory floor, added up over the length of the apprenticeship:

| Duration | Start 2026 | Start 2027 | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 years | €30,660 | **€32,364** | +€1,704 |
| 3.5 years | €36,744 | **€38,784** | +€2,040 |

*Calculation: monthly rate × 12 per training year; for 3.5 years, six months of year four. Gross pay, excluding employer social-security contributions.*

Pay is only part of the cost. According to the [BIBB cost-benefit survey](https://www.bibb.de/de/pressemitteilung_210430.php) (training year 2022/2023), an apprentice costs German companies around 26,200 euros gross per year on average. Besides pay, that includes the cost of training staff, chamber fees and learning material. ## Checklist for Training Companies

- **Check 2027 contracts:** if contracts starting in 2027 are already signed, the agreed amounts should reach at least 764 / 902 / 1,031 / 1,070 euros.
- **Clarify collective agreements:** if you are bound, the agreement applies. If not, factor in the 20 percent limit against the relevant agreement.
- **Plan each intake separately:** each intake keeps the table of its start year. An overview per intake prevents payroll errors.
- **Calculate part-time separately:** pay may be cut at most in proportion to the reduced hours.
- **Note the 2028 budget:** rates for apprenticeships starting in 2028 will be published by November 2027.

## Where the Bigger Cost Lever Is

Pay is set by law or by collective agreement. What companies can influence is the time trainers spend on administration, such as reviewing and signing off training records.
