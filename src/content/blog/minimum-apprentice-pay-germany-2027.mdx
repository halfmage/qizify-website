Anyone who starts an apprenticeship in Germany in 2027 is entitled to at least **764 euros** a month in the first year. That has been in the Federal Law Gazette since 7 October 2026.

For budgeting, that number is not enough. The floor depends on the **year the apprenticeship started**, not on the calendar year. An apprentice who started in August 2026 does not move to the new table in 2027.

## The 2027 Rates at a Glance

Monthly minimum pay under Section 17 (2) BBiG, by year the apprenticeship started ([2027 announcement](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bbimindvergfbek_2027/BJNR11F0A0026.html), [2026 announcement](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bbimindvergfbek_2026/BJNR0EB0A0025.html)):

| Training year | Start 2026 | Start 2027 | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | €724 | **€764** | +€40 |
| Year 2 | €854 | **€902** | +€48 |
| Year 3 | €977 | **€1,031** | +€54 |
| Year 4 | €1,014 | **€1,070** | +€56 |

Years two to four are not set separately: the law adds 18, 35 and 40 percent to the year-one amount.

## The Start Year Decides

The new amount only applies to apprenticeships **that begin in the year of the update** (Section 17 (2) BBiG). Each intake keeps its own table until the end of the apprenticeship.

For planning, this means: new contracts starting in 2027 must meet the new rates. The announcement does not change existing contracts. And this is only the legal floor. Whatever the apprenticeship contract or a collective agreement says applies regardless.

## Does the Minimum Wage Apply to Apprentices?

No. The Minimum Wage Act (MiLoG) explicitly does not cover the pay of people in vocational training ([Section 22 (3) MiLoG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/milog/__22.html)). Apprentices in training under the BBiG are covered by the minimum apprentice pay instead.

Which limit applies to your company depends mainly on collective agreements:

- **Bound by a collective agreement:** the agreement applies, even if it is below the minimum (Section 17 (3) BBiG).
- **Not bound:** pay may as a rule fall no more than 20 percent below the collective agreement that covers the apprenticeship (Section 17 (4) BBiG). This applies on top of the floor.
- **Part-time:** pay may not drop by a higher percentage than working time (Section 17 (5) BBiG). At 75 percent of normal hours, that is at least 573 euros in year one.

If in doubt, ask your chamber (IHK or Handwerkskammer) which collective agreement applies to your sector and region.

## What an Apprenticeship Starting in 2027 Costs at Minimum

The legal floor, added up over the full apprenticeship:

| Duration | Start 2026 | Start 2027 | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 years | €30,660 | **€32,364** | +€1,704 |
| 3.5 years | €36,744 | **€38,784** | +€2,040 |

*Calculation: monthly rate × 12 per training year; for 3.5 years, six months of year four. Gross pay, excluding employer social-security contributions.*

Pay is only part of the cost. According to the [BIBB cost-benefit survey](https://www.bibb.de/de/pressemitteilung_210430.php), gross costs in training year 2022/2023 were around 26,200 euros per apprentice per year, including training staff, chamber fees and learning material. ## Checklist for Training Companies

- **Check contracts starting in 2027:** at least 764 / 902 / 1,031 / 1,070 euros.
- **Clarify collective agreements:** if you are not bound, factor in the 20 percent limit.
- **Track each intake separately:** each with the table of its start year.
- **Note 2028:** the next rates will be published by 1 November 2027 at the latest.

## What Companies Can Influence

Pay is set by law or by collective agreement. What companies can influence is the time trainers spend on administration, such as reviewing and signing off training records. That is where the LearnSlice Training Logbook comes in: apprentices write and submit, trainers approve or send back for revision.
