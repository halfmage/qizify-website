# Research for `/de/verteidigungsindustrie` (13 Oct 2026)

Basis for the landing page copy. Two research passes: (1) Azubi pains at defence companies and suppliers, (2) search demand, competitors, trust expectations. Press sources are context; headline statistics on the page come from BIBB or other issuing bodies only.

## Key conclusions

1. **Search:** buyer searches barely exist. Every "Rüstungsindustrie/Verteidigungsindustrie" expansion is applicant or investor intent. The page converts campaign traffic (blog, LinkedIn, sales mails); organic traffic comes through the blog cluster. Realistic long-tail: "Ausbildung Rüstungsindustrie + KI / Lernplattform / Azubis", where no vendor ranks.
2. **Gap:** no vendor combines Azubi learning support + Ausbildungsrahmenplan + company documents + German/on-prem hosting + trainer early warning. SoftDeCC TCmanager ("LMS for Defense & Aviation") is compliance/certification tracking; generic AI tutors (ErfolgsAzubi etc.) have no defence angle. Chamber project MEKI (IHK Reutlingen/Stuttgart) validates the approach.
3. **No company we checked shows an AI tutor or LMS for Azubis** (Hensoldt "Learning Space" is an organisational unit). Position as an addition to the workshop and the trainers.
4. **Never write "VS-NfD-zugelassen/-konform".** Safe: "für Ausbildungsinhalte ohne Verschlusssachen; Ihr Geheimschutzbeauftragter entscheidet". Since 1 Sep 2025 firms self-accredit VS-NfD IT against the VS-NfD-Merkblatt (GHB Annex 4).
5. **Works council:** early-warning data is a §87 BetrVG topic; offer to set rules together (Betriebsvereinbarung), frame as support, not control.

Hosting location and who controls it, model origin, no training on customer data, AVV/TOMs, exit and deletion, clear pilot, German contact. ISO 27001 will be asked (competitors lead with it); per decision of 13 Oct 2026 it is not mentioned on the page and handled in the call. No price on the page.
