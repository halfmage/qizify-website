# SEO article briefs: defence campaign Q4 2026

One brief per article in the 13-week plan. Each brief fixes the reader, what they get, the scroll-stopping title and the opening lines, before anyone writes. If a brief has no clear reader value, the article does not get written.

## Rules for every article

### Value test (answer all four before writing)
1. **Who reads this?** One person, one situation (e.g. "Ausbildungsleiter at a 400-person supplier, second cohort of Mechatroniker starts in September").
2. **What can they do after reading that they could not before?** A decision, a plan, a conversation, a number.
3. **What do they take away?** Every article has one concrete asset: a table, checklist, timeline, template or calculation.
4. **Why us?** A fact they will not find on job boards or competitor blogs (BIBB data sheets, the timing of cancellations, export-control questions).

### Titles that stop the scroll (SERP and social)
- SEO title ≤ 60 characters, keyword in the first half. H1 can be longer and more human.
- Use one of: a number the reader did not know, a tension ("gleich viele Ausbilder, doppelt so viele Azubis"), a concrete promise ("90-Tage-Plan"), or a question the reader is already asking.
- No clickbait the article does not pay off. The title's promise is answered in the first screen.
- Meta description ≤ 155 characters: the result, not the topic.

### Structure that keeps people reading
1. **Opening, max 90 words:** a situation the reader recognises, the stakes, and what they get from this page. No "In der heutigen Zeit", no definitions.
2. **"Das Wichtigste in 30 Sekunden":** 3 bullets with the answer. People who only read this still leave with value; people who want more keep scrolling.
3. **The asset in the first third** (table, checklist, timeline). Do not bury the best part.
4. **H2s say what the reader gets**, e.g. "Monat 1 bis 4: Probezeit ist Entscheidungszeit", not "Hintergrund".
5. **One real example per section:** a named Beruf, a Lernfeld, a month, a situation in the workshop.
6. **Product late and light:** LearnSlice appears after the reader has had real value (not before 60 % of the text), max two mentions plus the closing CTA.
7. **Soft CTA mid-article** (calculator, checklist) and **demo CTA at the end** pointing to `/de/verteidigungsindustrie` or `/de/companies`.
8. **FAQ with 3 to 5 questions people actually ask** (check Google "Ähnliche Fragen"), answered in 2 to 4 sentences.
9. **Length follows content.** Spec is 1,800 to 2,400 words; if the reader is fully served at 1,500, stop. No padding.

### Human writing
- Write like an experienced Ausbildungsleiter explaining it to a colleague. "Sie", direct, calm, specific.
- Mix short and long sentences. Paragraphs 2 to 4 sentences.
- Banned: "In der heutigen schnelllebigen Welt", "Game-Changer", "nahtlos", "ganzheitlich", "revolutionieren", "Es ist wichtig zu beachten", "Zusammenfassend lässt sich sagen", "spielt eine entscheidende Rolle", chains of "Darüber hinaus", three-item lists in every paragraph, a rhetorical question as every H2.
- No em/en dashes, no " - " as a dash. Use colons, commas, full stops.
- Every number links to the issuing body (BIBB, DIHK, Destatis, gesetze-im-internet). Mark anything unverified before publishing, never guess.
- Read it out loud. If a sentence sounds like a brochure, rewrite it.
- Data wording: "vorzeitig gelöste Verträge", not "Abbruch", when quoting the Lösungsquote (the keyword "Ausbildungsabbruch" may appear in title and headings, the text explains the difference once).

## Week 1 · Pillar · `ausbildung-ruestungsindustrie`

- **Reader:** HR or training lead at a defence company or supplier; also applicants (fine, they share).
- **Value:** which trades the industry trains, how many start each year nationally, where contracts break, what that means for the company.
- **SEO title:** Ausbildung in der Rüstungsindustrie: 8 Berufe im Datencheck
- **H1:** Ausbildung in der Rüstungsindustrie: Welche Berufe gebraucht werden und wo Azubis verloren gehen
- **Meta:** Industriemechaniker, Mechatroniker, Elektroniker: Neuverträge, Prüfungserfolg und gelöste Verträge der 8 wichtigsten Berufe, mit BIBB-Daten.
- **Opening:** "Rheinmetall, Hensoldt, KNDS, Diehl: Die Verteidigungsindustrie hat im ersten Halbjahr 2026 rund 4.700 Stellen ausgeschrieben, 22 % mehr als im Vorjahr. Gebaut werden Panzer, Radare und Munition aber nicht von Ingenieuren allein, sondern von Mechatronikern, Zerspanern und Elektronikern. Genau in diesen Berufen wird jedes Jahr ein größerer Teil der Ausbildungsverträge vorzeitig gelöst. Hier sind die Zahlen, Beruf für Beruf."
- **Asset:** the 8-trade BIBB table (Neuabschlüsse 2025, Azubis, EQ I, Lösungsquote 2022 to 2024).
- **Outline:** Das Wichtigste in 30 Sekunden · Welche Berufe die Branche ausbildet (with company examples from careers pages) · Die Tabelle · Prüfung bestanden, Vertrag gelöst: was die Zahlen zeigen · Warum das erste Jahr entscheidet · Was Betriebe tun können · Sicherheit und Datenschutz bei digitalen Lernhilfen (short, link week 6) · FAQ
- **EN pair:** `apprenticeships-defence-industry-germany`, same data, written for international L&D readers.
- **New research to build in (checked 30 Sep 2026):**
  - *Pipeline squeeze (strongest new angle, make it an H2):* defence hiring +22 % (Index Research), but classic M+E apprenticeship contracts −9.1 % in 2025 and almost a fifth below 2019 (IG Metall Ausbildungsbilanz, 5 Mar 2026, on BIBB data; link BIBB where possible).
  - *No rescue from outside:* IAB (Weber/Yilmaz, 21 Sep 2026) counted only about 1,100 moves from other industries into defence in the past year; defence growth does not offset industrial job losses. Argument: companies have to grow and keep their own people.
  - *Shortage is real in exactly these jobs:* IW/KOFA Fachkräftereport (Jul 2026, March data): shortage gaps grow in M+E jobs (welding, metal processing, metal construction, machine building), driven by the defence and infrastructure special funds.
  - *Mismatch paradox:* 39,900 applicants still unplaced on 30 Sep 2025, highest since 2007 (BIBB Datenreport 2026). Companies lack apprentices while young people lack places: a matching and retention problem, not only a demographic one.
  - *Wording backed by BIBB itself:* Datenreport 2026 A5.6 states the Lösungsquote "ist keine Abbruchquote". Quote it in the "Lösung ist nicht gleich Abbruch" box.

## Week 2 · `ausbildungsabbruch-verhindern`

- **Reader:** Ausbilder/in who lost Azubis in year one and wants to prevent it next cohort.
- **Value:** a month-by-month plan for the first 12 months, with signals to watch and what to do.
- **SEO title:** Ausbildungsabbruch verhindern: Die ersten 12 Monate
- **H1:** Ausbildungsabbruch verhindern: Was in den ersten 12 Monaten passieren muss
- **Meta:** Zwei Drittel aller gelösten Ausbildungsverträge enden im ersten Jahr. Ein Monatsplan mit Warnsignalen und konkreten Schritten für Ausbilder.
- **Opening:** "Die meisten Azubis, die gehen, gehen früh. Zwei Drittel aller vorzeitig gelösten Ausbildungsverträge enden in den ersten zwölf Monaten, mehr als ein Drittel schon in der Probezeit. Wer erst im zweiten Lehrjahr gegensteuert, kommt oft zu spät. Dieser Plan zeigt, worauf Sie in welchem Monat achten."
- **Asset:** 12-month timeline table (Monat, typische Situation, Warnsignal, was Sie tun).
- **Outline:** 30 Sekunden · Wann Verträge gelöst werden (BIBB A5.6) · Lösung ist nicht gleich Abbruch · Der 12-Monats-Plan · Die häufigsten Gründe (BIBB research, verify source) · Gespräche, die wirken · Wo digitale Lernbegleitung hilft und wo nicht · FAQ
- **Sources:** BIBB Datenreport 2026 A5.6 (replaces 2025): 2024: 159,024 contracts cancelled early, Lösungsquote 29.7 %; 34.7 % of cancellations during probation, 32.9 % later in year one; in Industrie und Handel 36.5 % in probation.
- **New research to build in (checked 30 Sep 2026):**
  - *Why they think about quitting (make it the core H2):* Bertelsmann Stiftung "Ausbildungsperspektiven 2026" (n = 1,325 apprentices, 11 Mar to 20 Apr 2026): 23 % seriously considered quitting (44 % among those who quit before). Reasons among the 467 with quitting thoughts or experience: mental strain 36 %, training different from expected 30 %, conditions 29 %, no time/work-life balance 28 %, teaching quality 24 %, trade no longer liked 24 %, trainer conflict 22 %, better alternative only 13 %.
  - *The paradox to open with or close on:* the same study finds 90 % satisfied with their training overall and half "very satisfied" with their trainer. Satisfaction and quitting thoughts coexist: about a quarter feel emotionally burdened and a fifth overwhelmed often or always.
  - *Implication for the 12-month plan:* the expectation gap (30 %) is fixable before day one (realistic preview, Praktikum, first-week buddies); mental strain needs people and clear escalation paths; theory overload is where digital learning support fits. Be explicit that an app is not mental-health support.
  - *Validation from the chambers:* BIBB-funded project MEKI (IHK Reutlingen, IHK Region Stuttgart, Uni Stuttgart, TU and LMU München, end 2024 to end 2027) builds AI support for school content in trades with high cancellation rates and skills shortages, starting with IT-Systemelektroniker (Datenreport 2026, C3). Mention as a sign the approach is being taken seriously, not as a competitor attack.

## Week 3 · `mechatroniker-ausbilden`

- **Reader:** Ausbilder/in for Mechatroniker, often an experienced Meister with little time for theory support.
- **Value:** where Mechatroniker Azubis typically get stuck in the first two years, mapped to the Lernfelder, with what helps for each.
- **SEO title:** Mechatroniker ausbilden: Wo Azubis hängen bleiben
- **H1:** Mechatroniker ausbilden: Wo Azubis im ersten Jahr hängen bleiben und was hilft
- **Meta:** Elektrotechnik, Steuerungstechnik, Mathe: die Stolpersteine der Mechatroniker-Ausbildung nach Lernfeld, mit Tipps für Ausbilder.
- **Opening:** "Ein Mechatroniker muss in dreieinhalb Jahren zwei Berufe lernen: Metall und Elektro. Im ersten Jahr kommt beides gleichzeitig, dazu Steuerungstechnik und Mathe. Für viele Azubis ist das der Moment, in dem die Theorie davonläuft. Diese Übersicht zeigt, wo es typischerweise hakt und was Sie als Ausbilder konkret tun können."
- **Asset:** table Lernfeld · typischer Stolperstein · was hilft.
- **Verified facts (30 Sep 2026):** KMK Rahmenlehrplan Mechatroniker: year 1 = learning fields 1 to 5, 320 hours (LF1 Funktionszusammenhänge 40 h, LF2 mechanische Teilsysteme 80 h, LF3 elektrische Betriebsmittel unter Sicherheitsaspekten 100 h, LF4 Energie- und Informationsflüsse elektrisch/hydraulisch 60 h, LF5 Datenverarbeitung 40 h). MechatronikerAusbV § 6: Teil 1 "Arbeiten an einem mechatronischen Teilsystem" before the end of year two; § 8: weighting 40 %. Teil 2: Arbeitsauftrag 30 %, Arbeitsplanung 12 %, Funktionsanalyse 12 %, WiSo 6 %.
- **New research to build in:**
  - *AI can make apprentices faster without making them understand (key H2):* BIBB experiment (Merkel/Dörpinghaus, Datenreport 2026 C3.3), 27 second-year Fachinformatik apprentices: with ChatGPT they solved more tasks, faster, with less frustration, but showed no advantage in understanding code (worse on some tasks); many copied AI output unchecked. Transfer to Mechatronik with care: it is an IT study, say so.
  - *So the design question is Socratic vs answer machine:* a tutor that asks first and points to the curriculum source, versus a chatbot that hands out solutions. Offer trainers 3 test questions to check any AI tool.
  - *Defence itself learns this way:* Quantum Systems trains Bundeswehr drone operators with its own LMS and distance learning, cutting practical training to ten working days, about 20 % via simulator (hartpunkt, 11 Dec 2024). Use as a short example that blended learning is already normal in the industry.
  - *Shadow AI (one paragraph, links to week 6):* 60 % of employees who use AI say their tools were not officially introduced by the employer (DiWaBe 2.0, Datenreport 2026 C5.3). Apprentices will use AI anyway; the question is which one.
